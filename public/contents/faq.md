## What is SantiBet?

SantiBet is a Nigerian platform where you predict the outcome of real world events, including sports, politics, entertainment, crypto, and business, and win when your prediction is correct.

## Who can use SantiBet?

You must be 18 years of age or older, and able to legally participate in prediction and betting activity in your location, to open a SantiBet account.

## How do I sign up?

Create an account using your email address or phone number, verify your identity, and you are ready to fund your wallet.

## Why do I need to verify my identity?

Identity verification confirms you meet the minimum age of 18, protects your account from fraud, and is required under Nigerian anti-money laundering regulations.

## What currencies can I use?

You can deposit and withdraw using Naira, USDT, or USDC. You are not required to withdraw in the same currency you deposited in.

## How does predicting work?

Pick YES or NO on a market and enter your stake. When the market resolves, everyone who predicted correctly shares the losing side's total stake proportionally, credited straight to their wallet.

## What happens if a market is postponed or the outcome is unclear?

Your stake is returned to your wallet in full. You are never left without your money on a market that cannot be resolved fairly.

## When do I get paid?

Payouts are automatic and are credited to your wallet as soon as a market is resolved.

## Is my money safe?

Your wallet balance is tracked securely, and every deposit and withdrawal goes through a verified payment partner.

## Is SantiBet legal in Nigeria?

SantiBet is operated by AWA LAWA LIMITED under an Operational Accord with the Ekiti State Lotteries and Gaming Commission.

## How do I deposit?

Go to your wallet, choose Deposit, select Naira, USDT, or USDC, and follow the steps for that payment method.

## How do I withdraw?

Go to your wallet, choose Withdraw, select your currency, and confirm. Funds are sent to your verified bank account, card, or crypto wallet.
