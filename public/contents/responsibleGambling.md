## Play within your means

Santibet is built for engaging with real-world events — not as a way to solve financial problems. We offer tools to help you stay in control.

## Deposit limits

Set a daily, weekly, or monthly cap on how much you can add to your account. Once reached, deposits pause until the period resets.

## Cool-off period

Temporarily lock your account for 24 hours, 7 days, or 30 days. During this time you cannot place new positions or deposit.

## Self-exclusion

Permanently or long-term exclude yourself from Santibet. This action cannot be undone before the period you select has elapsed.

## Need support?

If gambling is affecting your life, reach out to a confidential helpline in Nigeria or contact our support team for guidance.
