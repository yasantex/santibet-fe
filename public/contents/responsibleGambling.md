Predicting on SantiBet is meant to be fun. It is entertainment, not a way to earn a living or solve a money problem. This page sets out how we support responsible play, and what to do if predicting stops feeling fun.

## Our age policy

SantiBet is strictly for users aged 18 and above. We verify age during registration, and we may request identity documents at any time to confirm this. Accounts found to belong to anyone under 18 will be closed and any balance handled in line with applicable law.

## Signs that predicting may be becoming a problem

- Staking more than you planned, or more than you can comfortably afford to lose.
- Increasing your stake to try to win back money you have already lost.
- Using money set aside for rent, food, school fees, or other essentials to fund your wallet.
- Thinking about predictions constantly, or feeling irritable when you are not able to predict.
- Hiding how much you deposit or how often you predict from people close to you.
- Borrowing money to fund your SantiBet wallet.

## Tools SantiBet gives you

- **Deposit limits.** Set a maximum amount you can add to your wallet in a day, a week, or a month, from Account Settings.
- **Reality check reminders.** Turn on periodic reminders showing how long you have been active on the platform and how much you have staked in that session.
- **Take a break.** Pause your account for a set period of your choosing, during which you cannot deposit or predict.
- **Self-exclusion.** Close your account to SantiBet for an extended period. Once activated, self-exclusion cannot be reversed early, including by contacting support, because it is designed to protect you even from a change of mind in the moment.

## A few practical habits

- Decide what you are willing to stake before you start, and stop when you reach that amount, win or lose.
- Treat any winnings as a bonus, not as income to plan around.
- Take regular breaks rather than predicting continuously.
- Never predict to relieve stress, boredom, or a difficult emotion.

## If you need support

Our Support contact and help line will be here.

If predicting is affecting your finances, your relationships, your work, or your wellbeing, please speak to a healthcare professional or a trusted person close to you. Support is available, and reaching out is a sign of strength, not weakness.
