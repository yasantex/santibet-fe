These terms apply to the **100% First Deposit Bonus** (up to ₦10,000), alongside our [Terms of Service](/terms-of-service).

## How you get it

- Make your first deposit of **₦1,000 or more** with the bonus box ticked (it is ticked for you). We match it 100% in bonus credit, up to ₦10,000.
- If you untick the box, the bonus is declined for good on that deposit.
- It is once per person. We check your phone number, device and payment method.
- You must be 18 or older. Staff accounts and suspended accounts can't get it.
- If you still have the ₦1,010 Free Deposit, your first deposit ends it.

## Verify your identity first

You need to verify your identity (KYC) before you can bet with this bonus. Until then, your bonus is shown but can't be used. It becomes active as soon as your ID is approved.

## How it works

- The bonus is **bonus credit**. It sits in your bonus balance, apart from your cash, and can't be withdrawn.
- Turn on **Use bonus** on the bet slip to stake it.
- When a bonus bet wins, the stake is used up and the **profit** goes to your **locked winnings**.
- **Once you have bet your bonus credit in full (1×), your locked winnings are released to your cash**, up to ₦10,000.
- If the bonus credit left over is too small to bet and you have won something, the bonus completes and your locked winnings are released.

## Which bets count

- Bonus bets are placed at the current price (no limit orders) and can't be cashed out early.
- The option you back must have a chance between 10% and 85%.
- The market must close at least 1 hour after you bet. **Live (in-play) markets can't use bonus.**
- You can't back both sides of the same market with your bonus, or bet your bonus against a bet you placed with your own cash.
- Combo bets can't use bonus credit.

## Expiry

- The bonus lasts **14 days** from when it was added. We'll remind you 2 days before it ends.
- When it expires, any bonus credit and locked winnings left are removed.
- Bonus bets that are still open when the bonus ends will settle, but won't pay anything.
- We may void a bonus that is abused.

## Withdrawing

Cash released by a bonus can be withdrawn once your identity is verified.
