AWA LAWA LIMITED, operating as SantiBet, respects your privacy. This page explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the rights you have over it under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

## Information we collect

- Account information: your full name, email address, phone number, and date of birth, used to confirm you meet the minimum age of 18.
- Identity verification information: a government issued ID and any supporting documents needed to confirm who you are, in line with Nigerian anti money laundering requirements.
- Financial information: deposit and withdrawal records, wallet balances, and the payment method you use, such as your bank account or crypto wallet address.
- Activity information: the predictions you place, the markets you view, and how you use the platform.
- Technical information: your device type, IP address, browser, and app version, used to keep SantiBet secure and working properly.

## Why we collect it

- To open, run, and secure your SantiBet account.
- To confirm your age and identity, and to meet our legal obligations under Nigerian gaming and anti money laundering law.
- To process your deposits, withdrawals, and payouts correctly and on time.
- To send you account related messages, such as deposit confirmations or a market you predicted on being resolved.
- To detect and prevent fraud, multiple account creation, and other abuse of the platform.
- To understand how SantiBet is used, so we can improve it.
Who we share your information with
- Our payment partners, including Nomba and Quidax, so they can process your deposits and withdrawals.
- Regulators, including the Ekiti State Lotteries and Gaming Commission, and law enforcement, where we are legally required to share information.
- Service providers who help us run the platform, such as identity verification and hosting providers, under confidentiality obligations.

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

## How we protect your information

We use industry standard security measures, including encryption of sensitive data and restricted internal access, to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

## Your rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023

- The right to know what personal data we hold about you.
- The right to request a copy of your data.
- The right to ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
- The right to request deletion of your data, subject to our legal record keeping obligations under Nigerian financial and gaming regulations.
- The right to object to certain uses of your data.

To exercise any of these rights, email info@santibet.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and in line with applicable law.

## How long we keep your information

We retain your information for as long as your account is active, and for a further period afterward as required by Nigerian financial and gaming record keeping rules.

## Children

SantiBet is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will note the date of the most recent update at the top of this page and highlight any significant change.
