## What we collect

We collect the information you provide during sign-up and KYC verification (name, date of birth, phone number, government ID), plus transaction and activity data needed to operate your account.

## How we use it

Your data is used to verify your identity, process deposits and withdrawals, prevent fraud, and comply with Nigerian financial regulations. We never sell your personal data to third parties.

## Data security

All personal and financial data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is limited to authorized personnel for verification and support purposes only.

## Your rights

You can request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account at any time by contacting support@santibet.com.
