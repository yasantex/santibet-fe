This page explains how santibet.com and the SantiBet app use cookies, and what choices you have.

## What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file that a website saves on your phone or computer. It lets the site remember things about your visit, such as whether you are logged in, so you do not have to re-enter information every time you open the app.

## The types of cookies we use

- **Strictly necessary cookies.** These keep you logged in, remember items in your session such as an unfinished deposit, and protect your account from unauthorised access. SantiBet cannot function properly without these.

- **Preference cookies.** These remember choices you have made, such as your preferred display currency (Naira, USDT, or USDC) and light or dark mode.

- **Performance and analytics cookies.** These help us understand which pages and markets are actually used, so we know where the platform is working well and where it needs improvement.

## What we do not do

We do not use cookies to build advertising profiles of you, and we do not sell cookie data to third-party advertisers.

## Managing your cookie preferences

When you first visit santibet.com, a cookie banner lets you accept or adjust which non-essential cookies you allow. You can also change cookie settings at any time through your browser or device settings. Turning off strictly necessary cookies will prevent core parts of SantiBet, such as staying logged in, from working correctly.

## Updates to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as SantiBet grows. Significant changes will be highlighted on this page.
