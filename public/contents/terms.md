## 2. How predicting on SantiBet works

- Each market presents a YES or NO question with a stated closing time.
- You choose a side and enter the amount you wish to stake.
- Once a market closes, no further stakes are accepted on that market.
- When a market is resolved, the total amount staked on the losing side is shared proportionally among everyone who staked on the winning side, and paid automatically into your wallet.
- If a market cannot be resolved fairly, for example because the underlying event is postponed or the outcome is genuinely unclear, your stake is returned to your wallet rather than being paid out or forfeited.
- A market can only be resolved once. Our systems are built to prevent a market being resolved a second time.
